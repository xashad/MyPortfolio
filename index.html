01 — About

The why behind the work

I'm a Master's student in AI for Science & Technology at the University of Milano-Bicocca, with a B.Tech in Computer Science from Lovely Professional University. My focus sits at the intersection of Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Explainable AI (XAI).

The thread through everything I build is trust. A model that predicts "high risk" or "defective" is useless if no one understands why — so I pair strong models (CNNs, Vision Transformers, gradient boosting) with explainability (Grad-CAM, LIME, SHAP, saliency, knowledge graphs).

I'm also a full-stack developer (React, Next.js, Supabase), which means I don't stop at a notebook — I ship models as live, interactive products. Currently building Lagani, an explainable stock-risk platform for Nepal's NEPSE market.
